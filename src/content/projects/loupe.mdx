## The Challenge

Design inspiration is everywhere and strangely useless. Pinterest boards, Dribbble shots and endless screenshot folders give you the picture but none of the information — *what font is that, which exact colors, who made it*. You can admire the work, but you can't learn from it or borrow the taste. And the category is owned by big incumbents, so a new gallery can't win on "we have screenshots too."

## My Approach

Inspobase is a hand-curated gallery where every piece carries the details that actually help. You paste a source link and the preview renders itself — a **website, app, image or video**. Each entry is tagged with its **fonts**, its **color palette** (shown as real swatches), its **style**, and **credit** to the maker. Browse by newest or popular, filter by type. Anyone can **submit** work, but everything lands in an **editorial approval queue** first — so the quality bar, which is the whole point, never drops.

## The Design

Light, clean and editorial — a white, content-forward canvas that puts the work first, built to go wide so more pieces show per screen. A **14-category sidebar** for real browsing, **live search** across titles, brands, styles and fonts, and a responsive **masonry grid where no two cards share a size** (up to four columns on large screens). Click any piece and it opens into an **immersive detail view** — a full-page capture you can scroll, prev/next navigation, click-to-copy color swatches, and a "more like this" rail — all deep-linkable so a single piece is shareable. **Featured** and **Sponsored** placements are built in for the business model, with a newsletter to compound the audience. A pluggable **Supabase** backend powers the shared gallery, submissions and saves, with a graceful offline fallback. It reads sharp from phone to tablet to desktop.

## Built with a partner

Inspobase is a collaboration: I lead engineering, my partner **[Ankit Yadav](https://saasexamples.com)** leads curation and distribution. The thesis is deliberately simple — in a space the incumbents already own, the edge isn't the code, it's **taste plus the metadata no one else bothers to capture**. The product design is production-grade and live; curation and features grow from here.
