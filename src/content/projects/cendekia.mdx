## The Challenge

A campus runs on two very different jobs. Administration needs a system of record — course registration (KRS), grades and transcripts, advising, and tuition (UKT) billing — where every number is correct and every role sees only what it should. Teaching needs the opposite: something light and motivating that students actually want to open. Bolting both onto one clunky portal is how most campus software ends up unloved.

## My Approach

I built Cendekia as two paired products that share one design language and one backend model. **SIAKAD** is the system of record: online KRS, KHS and transcripts, digital advising, and UKT billing, split cleanly across three roles — student, lecturer/advisor, and admin — with access enforced at the database, not just hidden in the UI. **The LMS** is the teaching side: auto-graded quizzes, XP, streaks and badges, and live attendance, designed to feel like an app rather than a form.

Both are built in Vue 3.5 + Vite 7 with Tailwind v4, backed by Supabase (Postgres, Auth and row-level security), and ship as installable PWAs so a student can add them to their home screen. There are no servers to babysit — everything runs on Supabase and the Cloudflare edge.

## The Results

Two deployed, role-based systems live at siakad.learningsystem.my.id and lms.learningsystem.my.id — the largest full-stack work I've shipped. Both run a **demo mode** seeded with representative personas (student, advisor, admin), so anyone can log in and click through without any real student data ever being involved.
