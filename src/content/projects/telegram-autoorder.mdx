## The Challenge

Selling digital products by hand doesn't scale — someone has to confirm each payment and send each item, around the clock. The hard part isn't the chat flow; it's making sure two buyers can't claim the last unit at the same moment, and that unpaid orders don't lock stock forever.

## My Approach

The bot runs the whole store in Telegram: browse, order, pay by QRIS, receive instantly — with no admin on shift. Stock is claimed atomically in Postgres with `SELECT ... FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`, so concurrent buyers can never oversell the same item, and unpaid orders auto-expire to release their hold. Built on Node 18 + Telegraf + Supabase.

## The Results

A self-running storefront that takes payment and delivers without a person in the loop — a clean showcase of the automation-engineering side of my work (and, at the time of writing, my most-starred public repo).
