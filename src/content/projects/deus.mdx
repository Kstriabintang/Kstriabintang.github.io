## The Challenge

Building a real company website means juggling architecture, design, copywriting, motion, SEO and QA — usually across several tools and several people. I wanted to see how far an AI-orchestrated pipeline could take that: one brief in, a coherent, opinionated site out, without the owner having to make a hundred low-level decisions.

## My Approach

Deus is a Claude Code plugin that runs a **crew of specialist agents** — architecture, design, copy, motion, SEO and QA — each with its own role, coordinated toward one output. Instead of asking the human for everything up front, it works through **curated, one-at-a-time decisions**: it proposes, you approve, it moves on. It's the clearest expression of how I actually work — spec-first, AI-orchestrated, human-verified.

## The Results

Deus is **early and honest about it** — it's on the first milestones of an eight-step roadmap, presented here as a product-in-progress rather than a finished tool. It's the strongest embodiment of the AI-engineering and LLM-orchestration side of what I do, and it's installable as a Claude Code skill today.
